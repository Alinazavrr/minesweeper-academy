My strongest idea: Minesweeper Academy + Arena
A platform where users don’t only play Minesweeper, they train, improve, compete, and understand probability.

Features That Would Look 3 Steps Ahead

Smart AI Coach

Probability overlay for risky cells.
Explains why a move is safe or dangerous.
After losing, shows: “Your mistake was here. Safer move was this.”
No-Guess / Guaranteed Solvable Mode

Boards are generated so every move can be solved logically.
This is a serious differentiator because many Minesweeper games rely on luck.
Replay System

Every game can be replayed step by step.
Users can share a replay link.
Great for leaderboards, learning, and showing off.
Daily Challenge + Seasons

Same board for everyone each day.
Rankings by time, mistakes, and accuracy.
Weekly/monthly seasons with badges.
Training Missions

Small puzzle levels teaching patterns:
1-2-1 pattern
1-2-2-1 pattern
corner logic
probability guesses
This makes it feel like Duolingo for Minesweeper.
Player Analytics

Win rate by difficulty.
Average solve time.
Flag accuracy.
Common mistake zones.
“You are faster than 72% of players in Medium.”
Multiplayer Race Mode

Two players get the same board.
First to solve wins.
Could be real-time or async ghost race.
Board Creator

Users can create custom boards and share them.
“Challenge your friend” links.
Community-made puzzle packs.
City / University Leaderboards

“Top players from Almaty”
“Top nFactorial players”
This matches the project description’s social layer and feels localized.
Product / Business Layer

Free: normal play, daily challenge, stats.
Pro: custom skins, advanced analytics, replay library, custom board packs.
You do not need real Stripe at first. Even a clean “Upgrade to Pro” modal shows business thinking.
Best MVP Combination

If you want maximum impact without building too much, I would implement:

Core Minesweeper
Auth + saved stats
Daily Challenge
Leaderboard
AI Coach / probability hints
Replay system
Training missions
Polished mobile UI
